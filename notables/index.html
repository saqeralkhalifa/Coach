In the spotlight

Dr. Khadija Adel

Intensive care doctor, four time IRONMAN 70.3 finisher and trail runner

Dr. Khadija Adel is a Bahraini intensive care doctor who races triathlon, runs trails and dives. Her bio lists four IRONMAN 70.3 finishes, in Salalah in 2022, Turkey in 2023 and Bahrain in 2023 and 2024, and marks both Bahrain races with a bronze medal. Published results show her finishing the Salalah race in 7:01:50. Away from racing she speaks in her own field, and in April 2025 she gave a talk at the first Bahrain anesthesia and pain conference.

By her own count she raced six times in 2025, with an age group win at the Cross Island race and a second place at Boom, a women's trail race in December that ended with a night in the desert, her first race of that kind. In October 2025 she ran above the clouds on Jabal Mobrah at 1,500 meters. In April 2026 she completed the duathlon of the Alfursan championship under the patronage of His Highness Shaikh Nasser bin Hamad Al Khalifa, racing alongside Abdulla Almoosa, who called her captain. The same month she shared a pair of photos from 2019 and 2026 and wrote that cycling had always been her favorite.

Follow @dr_khadija83 on Instagram

This week

News from the week to 22 November 2026

Bahrain's triathletes came back from Ras Al Khaimah with gold this week. Abdulrahman Mubarak posted that he won two gold medals at the Asian and West Asian Modern Pentathlon Championships, and wrote that he and his teammates from the national triathlon team took six golds at the Asian event, the first in Bahrain's history there.

Racing at home brought two more wins. Omar Hamza ran a 3 km leg in 9:18 for the BBK team that took first place in the NBK Bahrain Marathon Relay on 20 November and covered the 30 km in 1:34:44. A day later Christophe Sargis was eighth overall and first in the 50 to 59 category at the Bahrain half marathon, in 1h21.

On the bike, Roya Al Saati posted that she was second in her age group at the Škoda 18 km time trial, after finishing as the second woman overall in the CrediMax Grand Prix earlier this month.

About this page

Who is here, and why

Each person on this page is known to the triathlon community in Bahrain and the Gulf for what they have done in the sport over the years. Bahrainis come first, then citizens of the other Gulf states, then expatriates who have made the Gulf their racing home.

Instagram handles are shown where they have been confirmed, and more are added as they come in. If a detail is wrong or a name is missing, write to contact@arabianmethod.com.
