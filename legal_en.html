Privacy notice

How Arabian Method handles your personal data

Version 2026-11-11.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Dr. Saqer AlKhalifa, an individual resident in the Kingdom of Bahrain, who runs Arabian Method (the Arabian Method service) at arabianmethod.com. For any question or request about your data, email contact@arabianmethod.com or write through the contact page. This notice is given under the Personal Data Protection Law, Law No. (30) of 2018, and the resolutions issued under it.

2. What data is collected

  • Account data: your name, email address, country of residence and year of birth.
  • Training profile: your target race and date, your training days and preferred swim days, your years of training and weekly hours, the equipment you use, and any injury or health condition you choose to describe.
  • Health and training data: completed workouts (time, distance, heart rate and heart rate zones), sleep duration, resting heart rate, how you feel each day, and any report of pain, illness, fever or warning symptoms. This is sensitive personal data under the law.
  • Connection data: if you connect Intervals.icu, your Intervals.icu athlete number and an access token that lets Arabian Method read your activities and wellness data and write planned sessions to your calendar.
  • Coach conversations: the messages you write or dictate to your coach, the coach's replies and messages it starts, preferences you tell it, and how sessions felt. If you use voice, your own browser or phone turns your speech into text (Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari, under their own terms); Arabian Method receives only the text, never the recording.
  • Calendar busy times (optional): if you connect a calendar, the private address you paste and, from it, only the start and end of busy periods for the next three weeks and whether a period looks like travel. Titles, places, people and notes are never stored.
  • Ramadan setting (optional): whether you fast in Ramadan, the start date you confirm and, if you fast on some days, which days. Because this can indicate your religion, it is sensitive personal data and is used only with your explicit consent.
  • Heat setting (optional): whether you are new to hot weather, and the forecast heat and dust for each outdoor session, worked out for your country's capital city. Your precise location is never collected.
  • Notification address: if you turn on notifications, the technical address your browser or phone gives for delivering them.
  • Payment data: the payment method, amount, date and the reference you give for each BenefitPay or bank transfer payment. Arabian Method does not receive or store card or bank account numbers.
  • Fitness and race data: test and race results you enter, the training zones and race time estimates calculated from them, your sweat rate test result (sweat rate, your weight at the test and its date), your strength, summer mode and race conditions settings, your race plans, and a short note written after each completed workout. Notes are written by the AI system from workout numbers only, never from your name or anything you write about your health.
  • Consent records: which consents you gave or withdrew, the version of the text, and the time.

3. Why the data is used, and on what legal basis

Your data is used only to provide the coaching service: to write and adjust your weekly training plan, to send each session to your calendar and watch, to email you your daily session, to pause your plan and alert Dr. Saqer when you report pain, illness or warning symptoms, to answer your messages to the coach and make the plan changes you ask for, to send you messages from your coach and notifications, to adjust sessions for forecast heat and dust, to plan around your calendar and, if you choose, around Ramadan, to manage your membership, and to meet legal obligations.

Sensitive health data is processed on the basis of your explicit consent, given at registration and recorded. Your Ramadan setting is processed only on the basis of a separate explicit consent, given when you choose it, and withdrawing that consent clears the setting. Coach messages and the calendar connection can be switched off on your profile page at any time. Account and payment data are processed to perform the membership agreement you enter into. You can withdraw any consent at any time from your profile page. Withdrawing consent to the processing of health data, to international transfer, or to AI processing pauses your plan, because the service cannot run without them. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.

Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

4. How plans are written: automated processing

Each week an AI system (Claude, provided by Anthropic) proposes your training plan from your training data and check-ins. Software then checks every plan against fixed training rules and corrects or removes anything outside them. Dr. Saqer reviews and approves plans for the first group of athletes, and reviews every case that is flagged. Daily adjustments for short sleep or a raised resting heart rate are made automatically and only ever make a session easier. You can ask for any plan or adjustment to be reviewed by Dr. Saqer by writing to the contact address above.

The AI system does not receive your name, email address or the text you write about injuries or health conditions. It receives your age, race details, training days, training numbers and check-in numbers. The exception is the coach chat: the AI system receives the messages you write or dictate to the coach, which may include anything you choose to say, together with your plan, recent training, check-ins and calendar busy times, so that it can answer you. Plan changes made in the chat are checked against the same training rules as the weekly plans before they are saved. Changes for heat and dust are made by fixed rules and only make a session easier, shorter or indoor.

5. Who receives your data, and where

Your data is shared only with the service providers needed to run Arabian Method. Each processes it on Arabian Method's instructions:

  • Supabase, Inc.: database, logins and file storage, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Anthropic, PBC: the AI system that proposes plans and daily notes, in the United States. Anthropic does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models.
  • Resend, Inc.: email delivery, a United States company whose servers in Ireland send the emails. It receives your email address and the content of each email.
  • Open-Meteo: provides the weather, dust and sea temperature forecasts. It receives only the coordinates of your country's capital city or its coast, never any information about you.
  • Push notification services (Apple, Google or Mozilla, depending on your device): deliver notifications if you turn them on. Each notification is encrypted so that only your device can read it.
  • Your calendar provider (Google, Microsoft or Apple): only if you connect a calendar. Arabian Method reads the calendar from the address you give; the provider handles your calendar under its own terms.
  • GitHub, Inc.: hosts the website pages, in the United States. It does not receive your account or health data.
  • Intervals.icu: only if you connect it. Data passes between Arabian Method and your own Intervals.icu account at your request, and Intervals.icu handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. If your workouts come from a Garmin device, Garmin's own terms also apply.

Germany, Ireland and the United States are on the list of countries the Personal Data Protection Authority has recognized as providing adequate protection (Resolution No. (42) of 2022). Transfers to Intervals.icu are made at your request and with your explicit consent. Your data is not disclosed to anyone else unless the law requires it.

6. How long data is kept

Your data is kept while your account is open. Calendar busy times are replaced each day and deleted at once when you disconnect the calendar. Forecast conditions are deleted with the sessions they belong to. When you delete your account, your data is removed from the live systems at once and from backups within 30 days. Records of payments are kept, without any health data, for the period Bahraini law requires for financial records. Consent records are kept for the same period, so that consent can be shown to the Authority if asked.

7. Your rights

Under the law you may ask to be told whether your data is processed and to receive a copy of it, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have your data erased or blocked, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to object to a decision based solely on automated processing. Your profile page lets you download all your data, change your profile, withdraw consents and delete your account at any time. For anything else, write to the contact address above. Requests are answered within the period the law sets. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority.

8. Security

Passwords are stored only in hashed form. All connections are encrypted. Each athlete's data can be read only by that athlete and by Dr. Saqer, which the database enforces. Intervals.icu access tokens cannot be read from any browser. If a breach affects your data, Arabian Method will notify the Authority and you as the law requires.

9. Cookies and local storage

The site uses your browser's local storage only to keep you signed in and to remember your language, and, if you turn on notifications, a small background script that shows them. It uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

10. Changes to this notice

If this notice changes in a way that affects how your data is used, you will be told by email and asked for fresh consent where the law requires it.

Terms and medical disclaimer

Terms of membership and medical disclaimer

Version 2026-11-10.

1. The service

Arabian Method provides running and triathlon training plans written by an AI system within fixed training rules, supervised by Dr. Saqer AlKhalifa. It is a training service. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose, treat or advise on any medical condition.

2. Who may register

Registration is open to adults aged 18 and over. By registering you confirm that you are 18 or older and that the information you give is accurate.

3. Medical disclaimer

Endurance training carries a risk of injury, illness and, rarely, serious medical events. Before starting any plan you should be cleared to train by a doctor, particularly if you have a heart, lung or other medical condition, take regular medication, are pregnant, are returning from injury or illness, or have not trained regularly for some time.

Stop training at once and seek medical help if you have chest pain or pressure, fainting, dizziness, palpitations, unusual breathlessness, or any symptom that worries you. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Report pain, illness or warning symptoms in your daily check-in. Doing so pauses your plan and alerts Dr. Saqer. Your plan restarts only after you confirm that a doctor has cleared you to train. You remain responsible for deciding whether you are fit to train on any day, and for training within your own limits.

Heat is a serious risk in the Gulf and other hot climates. Train in the coolest part of the day, drink before and during sessions, and stop if you feel unwell.

4. Membership and payment

The first 30 athletes who confirm their email address receive their first month free. The free month starts on the first day of the week of their first approved plan, and no payment details are needed for it. After the free month, and for everyone else, the membership fee is US$20 a month, or the equivalent in another currency. Athletes pay by BenefitPay or by bank transfer, and any bank charges are paid by the sender. After paying, enter the payment reference on the Membership page. Your plan starts once the payment is confirmed. Each confirmed payment covers one month from the date your current membership ends, or from the date of confirmation if it has ended.

If a payment is not received by the end of your membership month, your plan pauses until it is. You can stop your membership at any time by not renewing it. Fees already paid for a month that has started are not refunded. If the fee changes, members are told by email at least 30 days before the change applies to them.

Keep your password private, keep your profile accurate, and complete your daily check-in honestly. Do not share your account.

7. Ending the service

You can delete your account at any time from your profile page. Dr. Saqer may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or where continuing would put the athlete at risk, and will tell the athlete why.
