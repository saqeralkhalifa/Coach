AI Coach

Triathlon and running plans that adjust to your week

Register, tell the coach about your race and the days you can train, and connect your watch. A new plan arrives each week, and each day's session is sent to your Garmin.

The science behind the plans

The rules every plan follows, and where they come from

The AI proposes each week, and software checks it against fixed rules taken from published research and established coaching methods. Anything outside the rules is corrected or removed before the plan reaches you.

Progression

Weekly running time rises by no more than 10%, and no single run is more than 10% longer than your longest run of the past 30 days. Sources: Frandsen and colleagues (2025), a study of 5,205 runners; Nielsen and colleagues (2014); Buist and colleagues (2008).Standing: one large cohort study for the single run limit. The weekly limit is more cautious than the research requires.

Periodization and taper

Plans build toward the race date, with a recovery week after every three load weeks (every two from age 50) and a taper of one to three weeks before the race. Sources: Bosquet and colleagues (2007) and Wang and colleagues (2023), meta-analyses of tapering studies; Mujika and Padilla (2003); Joe Friel.Standing: well established for the taper; established practitioner method for recovery weeks.

Intensity mix

At least 75% of training time is at low intensity, with no more than two hard sessions a week, spaced at least 48 hours apart. Sources: Seiler (2010); Stöggl and Sperlich (2014, 2015); Rosenblat and colleagues (2019).Standing: widely accepted. Researchers still debate a polarized against a pyramidal split, so plans allow either within limits.

Sleep and resting heart rate

Short sleep over several nights, or a resting heart rate well above your usual level, turns the next hard sessions into easy ones. Sources: Walsh and colleagues (2021), expert consensus in the British Journal of Sports Medicine; Buchheit (2014).Standing: expert consensus for sleep. The thresholds are set by the service, because the research gives no single number.

Heat

Sessions are planned for the coolest part of the day, and intensity is held down during the first two weeks of hot weather training. Sources: Racinais and colleagues (2015, 2023), consensus statements on training in the heat; American College of Sports Medicine (2021).Standing: expert consensus.

Coaching methods

Session design follows Joe Friel for triathlon and Jack Daniels for running.Standing: established practitioner methods used by coaches worldwide.

Left out on purpose

The acute to chronic workload ratio, which many apps use to predict injury, is not used.Its injury prediction claims have been seriously challenged in the research (Impellizzeri and colleagues, 2020 and 2021).

العلم وراء الخطط

القواعد التي تلتزم بها كل خطة ومصادرها

يقترح الذكاء الاصطناعي خطة كل أسبوع، ثم يتحقق منها برنامج وفق قواعد ثابتة مأخوذة من الأبحاث المنشورة ومناهج التدريب المعتمدة، ويُصحح كل ما يتجاوزها أو يُحذف قبل أن تصلك الخطة.

التدرج

لا يزيد وقت الجري الأسبوعي بأكثر من 10%، ولا تزيد أي حصة جري بأكثر من 10% على أطول جري لك خلال الثلاثين يوما الماضية. المصادر: Frandsen وزملاؤه (2025)، دراسة شملت 5,205 عدائين؛ Nielsen وزملاؤه (2014)؛ Buist وزملاؤه (2008).مستوى الدليل: دراسة أترابية كبيرة واحدة لحد الحصة الواحدة، والحد الأسبوعي أكثر تحفظا مما تتطلبه الأبحاث.

التحقيب والتخفيف قبل السباق

تُبنى الخطط تدريجيا نحو موعد السباق، مع أسبوع استشفاء بعد كل ثلاثة أسابيع حمل (وبعد كل أسبوعين من سن الخمسين)، وفترة تخفيف من أسبوع إلى ثلاثة أسابيع قبل السباق. المصادر: Bosquet وزملاؤه (2007) وWang وزملاؤه (2023)، تحليلان تجميعيان لدراسات التخفيف؛ Mujika وPadilla (2003)؛ Joe Friel.مستوى الدليل: راسخ بالنسبة للتخفيف، ومنهج تطبيقي معتمد بالنسبة لأسابيع الاستشفاء.

توزيع الشدة

لا يقل التدريب منخفض الشدة عن 75% من وقت التدريب، ولا تزيد الحصص الشاقة على حصتين في الأسبوع بفاصل لا يقل عن 48 ساعة. المصادر: Seiler (2010)؛ Stöggl وSperlich (2014، 2015)؛ Rosenblat وزملاؤه (2019).مستوى الدليل: مقبول على نطاق واسع. وما زال الباحثون يناقشون التوزيع المستقطب مقابل التوزيع الهرمي، لذلك تسمح الخطط بأي منهما ضمن حدود.

النوم ونبض الراحة

قلة النوم لعدة ليال، أو ارتفاع نبض الراحة بوضوح عن مستواك المعتاد، يحول الحصص الشاقة التالية إلى حصص سهلة. المصادر: Walsh وزملاؤه (2021)، إجماع خبراء منشور في المجلة البريطانية للطب الرياضي؛ Buchheit (2014).مستوى الدليل: إجماع خبراء بالنسبة للنوم. وتحدد الخدمة العتبات بنفسها، لأن الأبحاث لا تحدد رقما واحدا.

الحرارة

تُخطط الحصص لأبرد أوقات اليوم، وتبقى الشدة منخفضة خلال أول أسبوعين من التدريب في الطقس الحار. المصادر: Racinais وزملاؤه (2015، 2023)، بيانا إجماع حول التدريب في الحرارة؛ الكلية الأمريكية للطب الرياضي (2021).مستوى الدليل: إجماع خبراء.

مناهج التدريب

يتبع تصميم الحصص منهج Joe Friel في الترايثلون ومنهج Jack Daniels في الجري.مستوى الدليل: مناهج تطبيقية معتمدة يستخدمها المدربون حول العالم.

ما استُبعد عمدا

لا تُستخدم نسبة الحمل الحاد إلى المزمن التي تعتمد عليها تطبيقات كثيرة للتنبؤ بالإصابات.لأن ادعاءات قدرتها على التنبؤ بالإصابات تعرضت لطعن جدي في الأبحاث (Impellizzeri وزملاؤه، 2020 و2021).

Safety and privacy

Read before you register

Registration is open to adults aged 18 and over. Get clearance from a doctor before starting a new training plan. The coach does not give medical advice, and any report of pain, illness, chest symptoms or dizziness pauses your plan and is reviewed by Dr. Saqer.

Your data is used only to write your plans and is handled under Bahrain's Personal Data Protection Law. You can download it or delete your account at any time from your profile. Read the privacy notice and the terms and medical disclaimer.

          Privacy notice

          How AI Coach handles your personal data

          Version 2026-11-03.

          1. Who is responsible for your data

          The data controller is Dr. Saqer AlKhalifa, an individual resident in the Kingdom of Bahrain, who runs AI Coach at coach.saqeralkhalifa.com. For any question or request about your data, write through the contact page. This notice is given under the Personal Data Protection Law, Law No. (30) of 2018, and the resolutions issued under it.

          2. What data is collected

          • Account data: your name, email address, country of residence and year of birth.
          • Training profile: your target race and date, your training days and preferred swim days, your years of training and weekly hours, the equipment you use, and any injury or health condition you choose to describe.
          • Health and training data: completed workouts (time, distance, heart rate and heart rate zones), sleep duration, resting heart rate, how you feel each day, and any report of pain, illness, fever or warning symptoms. This is sensitive personal data under the law.
          • Connection data: if you connect Intervals.icu, your Intervals.icu athlete number and an access token that lets AI Coach read your activities and wellness data and write planned sessions to your calendar.
          • Payment data: the payment method, amount, date and the reference you give for each BenefitPay or bank transfer payment. AI Coach does not receive or store card or bank account numbers.
          • Consent records: which consents you gave or withdrew, the version of the text, and the time.

          3. Why the data is used, and on what legal basis

          Your data is used only to provide the coaching service: to write and adjust your weekly training plan, to send each session to your calendar and watch, to email you your daily session, to pause your plan and alert Dr. Saqer when you report pain, illness or warning symptoms, to manage your membership, and to meet legal obligations.

          Sensitive health data is processed on the basis of your explicit consent, given at registration and recorded. Account and payment data are processed to perform the membership agreement you enter into. You can withdraw any consent at any time from your profile page. Withdrawing consent to the processing of health data, to transfer outside Bahrain, or to AI processing pauses your plan, because the service cannot run without them. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.

          Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

          4. How plans are written: automated processing

          Each week an AI system (Claude, provided by Anthropic) proposes your training plan from your training data and check-ins. Software then checks every plan against fixed training rules and corrects or removes anything outside them. Dr. Saqer reviews and approves plans for the first group of athletes, and reviews every case that is flagged. Daily adjustments for short sleep or a raised resting heart rate are made automatically and only ever make a session easier. You can ask for any plan or adjustment to be reviewed by Dr. Saqer by writing to the contact address above.

          The AI system does not receive your name, email address or the text you write about injuries or health conditions. It receives your age, race details, training days, training numbers and check-in numbers.

          5. Who receives your data, and where

          Your data is shared only with the service providers needed to run AI Coach. Each processes it on AI Coach's instructions:

          • Supabase, Inc.: database, logins and file storage, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.
          • Anthropic, PBC: the AI system that proposes plans and daily notes, in the United States. Anthropic does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models.
          • Resend, Inc.: email delivery, a United States company whose servers in Ireland send the emails. It receives your email address and the content of each email.
          • GitHub, Inc.: hosts the website pages, in the United States. It does not receive your account or health data.
          • Intervals.icu: only if you connect it. Data passes between AI Coach and your own Intervals.icu account at your request, and Intervals.icu handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. If your workouts come from a Garmin device, Garmin's own terms also apply.

          Germany, Ireland and the United States are on the list of countries the Personal Data Protection Authority has recognized as providing adequate protection (Resolution No. (42) of 2022). Transfers to Intervals.icu are made at your request and with your explicit consent. Your data is not disclosed to anyone else unless the law requires it.

          6. How long data is kept

          Your data is kept while your account is open. When you delete your account, your data is removed from the live systems at once and from backups within 30 days. Records of payments are kept, without any health data, for the period Bahraini law requires for financial records. Consent records are kept for the same period, so that consent can be shown to the Authority if asked.

          7. Your rights

          Under the law you may ask to be told whether your data is processed and to receive a copy of it, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have your data erased or blocked, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to object to a decision based solely on automated processing. Your profile page lets you download all your data, change your profile, withdraw consents and delete your account at any time. For anything else, write to the contact address above. Requests are answered within the period the law sets. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority.

          8. Security

          Passwords are stored only in hashed form. All connections are encrypted. Each athlete's data can be read only by that athlete and by Dr. Saqer, which the database enforces. Intervals.icu access tokens cannot be read from any browser. If a breach affects your data, AI Coach will notify the Authority and you as the law requires.

          9. Cookies and local storage

          The site uses your browser's local storage only to keep you signed in and to remember your language. It uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

          10. Changes to this notice

          If this notice changes in a way that affects how your data is used, you will be told by email and asked for fresh consent where the law requires it.
